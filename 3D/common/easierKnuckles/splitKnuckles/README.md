This is another solution if "in place" printed knuckles can't fold, suggested by @FALLOURD (thanks!)

The knuckles are split in 3 parts that will be hold together while inserted in their respective finger slots.

The parts should be printed in this position:

<img width="640" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a0c08264-71ba-47fa-bb4a-cfd01f7b47fe" />


Parts should then be assembled this way before being pushed in the fingers and palm slots. If they seem too loose, a drop of glue might help.
